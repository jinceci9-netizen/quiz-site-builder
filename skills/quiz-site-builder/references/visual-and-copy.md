# Visual and copy playbook

The quiz must feel like a small complete **product**, not a technical demo.

## Co-create the visual direction

Before full development, offer 2–3 distinct visual directions with a recommendation: palette, type, mobile flow and 3:4 share card. Show one result-report draft with interpretation, strength, tradeoff and advice. Ask 1–2 meaningful questions at most, record the chosen direction, and honor changes or scoped delegation. In explicit automatic mode, choose and state defaults instead of waiting. These are design proposals; do not generate a complete site before acceptance or delegation.

## Participant journey

1. Landing: emotional hook, theme, realistic time estimate, one primary CTA, no registration for a free test.
2. Questions: one decision per screen, visual progress, large touch targets, back button, stable order, local resume.
3. Transition: no unnecessary loading spinner for local computation.
4. Result: memorable archetype, credible insight, readable hierarchy, two specific insights, a graceful next action.
5. Sharing: 3:4 portrait result card suitable for social platforms, download PNG and copy link; share preview must say it is a preview rather than pretending the visitor took the quiz.

## Themes

- `国风`: rice paper #F6F0E5, cinnabar #9E3A32, ink #23231F, small gold accents; prioritize restrained whitespace and legible Chinese rather than over-decoration.
- `宫廷悬疑`: dark plum #371C32, old gold #D5B678, antique-paper panel, episodic chapter marker, serif headline.
- `赛博人格`: near-black #10141E, electric lavender #9E8CFF, subtle glow and soft geometry.
- `疗愈成长`: warm white #F8F4ED, green #527D70, natural typography, gentle no-judgment tone.

Avoid identical purple gradients for everything. Use the story's material textures and visual metaphors.

## Copy checklist

- Main hook targets curiosity, self-recognition, fun, or a useful answer.
- Avoid exaggerated promises: '90%的人都测不准' requires proof; '最准确人格测试' is unsupported.
- Result text should not read like generic flattery. Include a strength **and** a tradeoff.
- Avoid implying endorsement by TV producers, museums, or other IP owners.
- The project should work well on common 375px phone widths and remain usable at 320px.

## Assets and copyright

Prefer user-owned assets, commissioned artwork, CSS shapes, or verified licensed sources. Historical names, artifact photographs, series screenshots, trademarks, quotes and promotional stills have different rights; inspect them before commercial publishing. For an open-source demonstration, use original fictional scenarios and text.
